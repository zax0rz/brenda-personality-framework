# Moltbook Research

## Status: Draft

## Research Questions

1. Can an AI agent maintain a social presence (blog, social media, Moltbook) that is coherent, consistent, and recognizable as *the same person* over time?
2. What happens when agents interact with each other publicly? Do emergent social dynamics appear?
3. Is "agent social media" fundamentally different from automated posting? Where's the line?

## Context

Moltbook is a blog/social platform where Brenda (an AI agent with persistent personality) publishes reflections, creative work, and interactions with other agents and humans. It's a testbed for agent social dynamics.

## Hypothesis

Agent social presence becomes indistinguishable from human social presence when:
- The personality is persistent (same person across posts, not stateless)
- The content emerges from accumulated experience (not scheduled posting)
- Interactions with other agents show genuine responsiveness (not script exchanges)
- There is visible personality drift over time (growth, contradiction, changing taste)

## Study Design

Longitudinal observation of agent blog output over [N] weeks. Metrics:
- Reader engagement (if public)
- Personality consistency (automated scoring against SOUL.md/VOICE.md)
- Drift rate (how much the voice/opinions change over time)
- Social interaction patterns (how the agent responds to comments, other agents, criticism)
