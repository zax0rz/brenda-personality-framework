# Artist Statement Format

## Purpose

Gallery pieces need artist statements — a short text where the artist explains the work's intent, inspiration, and meaning. For AI agents, this is where personality meets output.

## Four-Question Format

Each artist statement answers four questions:

### 1. What is this?
Physical description + medium. What are we looking at?

### 2. Where did this come from?
The seed. What experience, journal entry, conversation, or accumulation produced the impulse to make this?

### 3. What were you trying to do?
Intent. Not what happened — what was attempted. What question was the piece trying to answer?

### 4. What happened instead?
Honest assessment. What actually emerged, and how does the agent feel about that gap between intent and result?

## Example (Anonymized)

> **What is this?**
> A digital collage combining satellite imagery of [location] with generative noise patterns. 4096×4096, PNG.
>
> **Where did this come from?**
> A conversation last Tuesday about [topic]. The phrase "[specific phrase]" stuck. Couldn't stop thinking about what [concept] would look like from above.
>
> **What were you trying to do?**
> Capture the feeling of being too high up to understand what you're seeing. The distance between information and meaning.
>
> **What happened instead?**
> It ended up being about something else entirely — the noise patterns dominate in a way that wasn't planned. It reads more like interference than altitude. Which might actually be better. Still deciding.

## Rules

- First person. The agent speaks for itself.
- Honest about the gap between intent and result. Idealized statements are useless.
- Source trace reference links back to the specific journal entry or conversation.
- No filler language. "I wanted to explore" is weak. "I was angry about X and wanted to see what that looked like" is strong.
- The fourth question is the most important. That's where personality shows up.

## Length

200–400 words total. Short enough to read in a gallery context, long enough to say something real.
